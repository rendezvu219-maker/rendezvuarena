Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 August 2026

Public test warning

This is a public test website with limited security. Never reuse a password from email, banking, social media, games or another important account. Use a unique password only for this website.

Information processed

The service may process usernames, password hashes, tournament and roster data, pasted public tournament-profile links, draft-room activity, match chat, results, security logs and files voluntarily uploaded as evidence or attachments. Public test accounts do not collect an email address.

Why it is processed

Information is used to authenticate users, connect accounts with tournament entries, operate events, synchronise rooms, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and maintain service reliability.

Providers and international processing

Hosting and external tournament platforms may process limited information when users open their public links. The website does not request external-platform passwords and does not use OAuth for public test account linking.

Cookies and local storage

Authentication uses secure HTTP-only cookies. Interface preferences such as language and appearance may be stored in the browser. The service should not add analytics or advertising cookies without updating this policy and obtaining any required consent.

Retention and security

Tournament records, messages, evidence, session records and logs are retained only for the periods configured by the operator and reasonably needed for event administration, dispute handling, security and legal obligations.

Your requests

Users may ask the operator to access, correct or delete account information, subject to lawful retention and active dispute requirements.

Privacy contact: configure a monitored PUBLIC_CONTACT_EMAIL before launch.

No sale to advertisers

The project does not sell personal data to advertisers. This statement must be reviewed again if analytics, advertising or new integrations are enabled.
