Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 August 2026

Information processed

The service may process account identifiers, email-verification status, tournament and roster data, linked public tournament profiles, draft-room activity, match chat, results, security logs and files voluntarily uploaded as evidence or attachments.

Why it is processed

Information is used to authenticate users, verify email ownership, connect accounts with tournament entries, operate events, synchronise rooms, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and maintain service reliability.

Providers and international processing

Depending on operator configuration, hosting, transactional email, start.gg OAuth and other external platforms may process limited information. Their locations and retention practices must be reviewed by the operator before launch and disclosed where required.

Cookies and local storage

Authentication uses secure HTTP-only cookies. Interface preferences such as language and appearance may be stored in the browser. The service should not add analytics or advertising cookies without updating this policy and obtaining any required consent.

Retention and security

Tournament records, messages, evidence, session records and logs are retained only for the periods configured by the operator and reasonably needed for event administration, dispute handling, security and legal obligations. No deployment should launch until those periods and a deletion process are documented.

Your requests

Users may ask the operator to access, correct or delete account information, subject to lawful retention and active dispute requirements.

Privacy contact: configure a monitored PUBLIC_CONTACT_EMAIL before launch.

No sale to advertisers

The project does not sell personal data to advertisers. This statement must be re-reviewed if analytics, advertising or new integrations are enabled.
